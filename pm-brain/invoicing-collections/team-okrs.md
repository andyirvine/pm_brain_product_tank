# Invoicing & Collections: Team OKRs

**PM Owner**: Claire | **Area**: Invoicing & Collections | **Year**: 2024

## Team Mission

Make getting paid effortless and emotionally painless for small business owners. Remove every moment of friction between completing work and having money in the bank.

## Team OKRs — 2024

### O1: Make invoicing fast enough that owners never delay billing

**Why**: 8/10 IC interviews show owners delay invoicing — not because it's hard, but because it takes too long or feels awkward. Every day of delay is a day of DSO added.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: Average invoice creation time ≤ 2 minutes for returning clients**
- Baseline: ~15–30 minutes (manual, Word/Google Docs)
- Target: ≤ 2 minutes using saved client templates
- Measurement: In-app timing from "New Invoice" to "Send"

**KR2: 80% of users send invoice within 24 hours of work completion**
- Baseline: Unknown (pre-launch)
- Target: 80% of invoices sent within 24 hours of project close
- Measurement: Track invoice creation date vs. project end date (where known)

**KR3: Recurring invoice setup used by 60% of users with regular clients**
- Baseline: 0% (feature not yet launched)
- Target: 60% of users with ≥ 2 active clients enable recurring invoicing
- Measurement: Feature adoption tracking

---

### O2: Reduce the emotional friction of payment follow-up

**Why**: Shame and anxiety around chasing payment are the #1 emotional barrier in IC interviews (8/10 solopreneurs). Automation that feels professional removes the personal discomfort.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: 70% of users have automated payment reminders enabled**
- Baseline: 0% (feature not yet launched)
- Target: 70% opt-in rate for automated reminders
- Measurement: Feature settings adoption

**KR2: Average DSO drops from ~45 days to ≤ 30 days**
- Baseline: ~45 days (from interview evidence, e.g., Raj)
- Target: 30 days average across active users
- Measurement: Invoice date → payment received date, averaged monthly

**KR3: NPS on "payment follow-up experience" ≥ 7/10**
- Baseline: N/A (pre-launch)
- Target: 7+ out of 10 on in-app survey: "How comfortable do you feel following up on late payments?"
- Measurement: Quarterly NPS pulse survey

---

### O3: Give owners a real-time, complete picture of cash coming in

**Why**: 7/10 IC users couldn't answer "how much is outstanding right now?" without manual effort. Visibility drives behavioral change — owners follow up more and plan better when they can see clearly.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: 80% of MAU open the payment dashboard at least once per week**
- Baseline: 0% (feature not yet launched)
- Target: 80% weekly active use of dashboard
- Measurement: In-app engagement tracking

**KR2: Average monthly reconciliation time drops from ~3.5 hours to ≤ 30 minutes**
- Baseline: ~3–4 hours/month (Interview 002, 008)
- Target: ≤ 30 minutes with consolidated dashboard + integrations
- Measurement: Post-launch survey: "How long do you spend reconciling invoices and payments each month?"

**KR3: Users can answer "how much do I have outstanding?" in under 10 seconds**
- Baseline: Currently requires checking 2–3 tools manually
- Target: Outstanding balance visible on dashboard without any navigation
- Measurement: Usability testing (task completion time < 10 seconds)

## Quarterly Milestones

### Q1 (Jan–Mar): Foundation
- **Ship**: Payment status dashboard (MVP)
- **Ship**: Invoice creation with saved client templates
- **Target**: 50 beta users activated
- **Validate**: Invoice creation time ≤ 2 minutes for returning clients
- **Success signal**: 7+ NPS on invoice creation experience

### Q2 (Apr–Jun): Automation
- **Ship**: Recurring invoice scheduling
- **Ship**: Automated payment reminders (with tone customization)
- **Target**: 100 paying customers
- **Measure**: DSO baseline → track improvement
- **Validate**: Reminder opt-in rate; measure DSO impact

### Q3 (Jul–Sep): Expansion
- **Ship**: Client payment pattern tracking ("This client typically pays in 45 days")
- **Ship**: Agency features — team permissions, milestone billing
- **Design**: Accountant read-only access (for Q4)
- **Target**: 200 customers; 60%+ on agency plan using dashboards

### Q4 (Oct–Dec): Ecosystem
- **Ship**: Accountant integration (read-only client view)
- **Ship**: API integrations (Stripe, QuickBooks, bank sync)
- **Target**: 10 accountant partners onboarded
- **Measure**: Full-year DSO improvement; referral impact from accountants

## What We're Not Building in 2024

- **Inventory or COGS tracking** — product businesses; different problem domain
- **Contract or legal workflows** — out of scope; too complex
- **Time-tracking integration** — deferred to 2025 (agency expansion)
- **Mobile-first invoicing** — web first; mobile in backlog
