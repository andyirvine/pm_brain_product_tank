## The Problem: Project-Based Invoicing

**James's pain point**: "With projects, I need to invoice at milestones, not completion. I might have work across January and February, but I invoice at specific gates: design approval, development start, launch."

**The complexity**: Harvest tracks his team's time, but he needs invoices to align with project milestones, not time spent. This mismatch creates manual reconciliation.

### Example

**Project**: Website redesign, $18K total
- **Milestone 1 (design approval)**: Invoice $6K
- **Milestone 2 (dev start)**: Invoice $6K  
- **Milestone 3 (launch)**: Invoice $6K

**Reality**: Team has worked 150 hours across 8 weeks. Mapping that to 3 milestone invoices requires manual consolidation—the tool doesn't do it automatically.

**Old approach**:
1. Harvest shows 150 hours of time entries
2. James manually consolidates into 3 milestone invoices
3. Calculates what work maps to what milestone (subjective)
4. Creates invoices in QB (outside of time tracking)
5. Result: 2-3 hours per project in manual work

---

## How Ledger Helped

**James**: "Ledger lets me create invoices that don't align to time entries. I can invoice based on milestones, not hours. That's perfect for my business model."

### New Workflow

1. Project starts in Monday.com (with milestone dates)
2. Team logs time in Harvest (for payroll/tracking)
3. At milestone, James creates invoice in Ledger (amount: $6K, milestone: "Design approval")
4. Ledger shows which milestone has been invoiced (prevents double-invoicing)
5. Links back to project in Monday.com
6. Sends to client

**Impact**: Reduced manual reconciliation from 2-3 hours per project to 30 minutes.
