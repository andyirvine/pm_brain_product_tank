**Annual Revenue**: ~$85K | **Company Size**: Solo | **Years in Business**: 5

---

## The Problem: Tax Season Panic

Emma runs an online fitness coaching business ($85K annual revenue). She's profitable but completely stressed about taxes.

**Emma's pain**: "Every March, I panic. I have receipts in three different places, invoices scattered, and no idea what my tax liability is. I end up scrambling to pull everything together."

### Current Process
1. Year-end: Tries to collect all receipts (some in email, some in Venmo, some physical)
2. Realizes records are a mess
3. April 15: Paying accountant $500-800 for cleanup + filing
4. Pays balance owed (often a surprise)

**Impact**: 
- Stress/anxiety (8-week window of tax panic)
- Expensive cleanup cost
- No visibility into tax liability throughout year
- Conservative spending decisions due to tax uncertainty

### The Anxiety

**Emma**: "I'm anxious about taxes because I don't understand my obligations. What if I'm underpaying estimated taxes? What if I owe a huge penalty? I just want clarity."

---

## What Emma Wants

1. **Quarterly tax estimates**: "How much should I set aside each month?"
2. **Expense tracking**: "Which expenses are deductible?"
3. **Year-round visibility**: "Not a surprise in March"
4. **Education**: "Explain tax obligations in plain English"

---

## Hypothesis

**Ledger Hypothesis**: "Quarterly tax estimates reduce March panic and improve business decisions"

✅ **Strongly validated**: Emma explicitly says quarterly visibility would change her behavior (less conservative spending, less anxiety).
