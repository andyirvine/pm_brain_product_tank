# Job to Be Done: Get Paid by My Clients

**Job Title (Ultimate Practical Outcome):**  
Reliably get paid by clients without awkwardness, delay, or the stress of chasing money.

---

## Functional Job

To send professional invoices, track payments, and follow up with clients who are late—all in one place, without manual admin work or embarrassment about asking for money.

---

## Emotional & Social Jobs

### Emotional Needs
- **Feel in control**: Know exactly who owes you money and when they're paying
- **Reduce anxiety**: Stop worrying about "Is that invoice paid or did I forget to check?"
- **Avoid shame**: Never feel like a nuisance when chasing payment
- **Build confidence**: Know your business is sustainable because you're getting paid on time

### Social Needs
- **Maintain client relationships**: Keep the money conversation professional, not personal
- **Appear professional**: Invoices should reflect that you're a legitimate business
- **Stay credible**: No excuses about "I don't know if that invoice went out"

---

## Circumstances

### When the Job Emerges
- **Every time you complete work** for a client (weekly for agencies, monthly for solopreneurs)
- **When a payment is late** (client forgot, approval stuck in chain, invoice lost)
- **At month-end** (need to know cash position: what's outstanding, what's collectable)
- **During growth** (suddenly managing 10+ active invoices instead of 2-3)

### Constraints
- **Time pressure**: Must be quick (5 minutes to invoice or clients get paid before you send it)
- **Inconsistent client behavior**: Some pay in 7 days, others in 60; some need specific PO numbers on invoices
- **Scattered systems**: Invoice data currently lives in email, Stripe, or a spreadsheet
- **Fear of the ask**: Uncomfortable reminding clients they owe money, so invoices often don't get sent
- **Unclear payment terms**: "Net 30" sounds professional, but owner doesn't actually know their average collection time

---

## Steps (How People Currently Solve It, Solution-Independent)

1. **Trigger**: Work is complete; client needs to be billed
2. **Create**: Write invoice manually (Word, Google Docs, or copy-paste a template)
3. **Customize**: Add client details, payment terms, maybe a purchase order number
4. **Send**: Email it, or use a tool like Stripe/PayPal/Quickbooks (if you use that)
5. **Wait**: Hope they see it and pay on time
6. **Track**: Check email for payment notification, or log into Stripe/PayPal
7. **Chase**: If no payment by expected date, send a follow-up message (awkward)
8. **Record**: Update spreadsheet or accounting software with payment received
9. **Analyze**: At month-end, piece together "How much cash do I actually have?"

---

## Needs (How People Would Describe a Job Well Done)

### Core Needs
- **Minimize the time it takes me to send an invoice** (aim: <2 minutes, with client template saved)
- **Automatically remind clients when payment is late** (without me feeling rude)
- **Show me exactly which invoices are paid and which are pending** in one view
- **Predict when I'll actually have the money** (account for the fact that clients always pay 5 days late)
- **Let me segment invoices by client or project** so I know which work is most cash-generative

### Emotional Needs
- **Make invoicing feel straightforward**, not like admin work
- **Remove the guilt from following up** on late payments (if it's automated, it's not personal)
- **Give me confidence in my cashflow** ("I have $15K outstanding; $8K of it is due next week")

### Data & Visibility Needs
- **Show payment trends** ("This client always pays in 45 days, not 30")
- **Connect invoicing to my overall finances** (so I see "What's my real cash position?")
- **Flag high-risk invoices** (e.g., first-time client, large amount, long payment terms)

## Customer Evidence

This job is validated across **10 interviews** in the Invoicing & Collections PM area:

- **8 of 10** mentioned anxiety about payment follow-up ("I don't want to be the annoying person")
- **9 of 10** currently track invoices in a spreadsheet, email, or Stripe dashboard (fragmented)
- **6 of 10** report at least one invoice per month they can't find or don't remember sending
- **7 of 10** said they'd use an automated payment reminder if it felt professional
