# Interview 011: Emma Garcia
**Segment**: Solopreneur | **Business**: Coaching Business (Life Coaching) | **Status**: Non-user (DIY) | **Date**: Mar 2024

---

## Background
**Business Model**: 1:1 coaching + group workshops  
**Annual Revenue**: ~$95K | **Years in Business**: 4 | **Current Tools**: None (no accounting system); Stripe for payments; Quickbooks Excel template

---

## The Problem

**Primary Pain**: Tax season is chaotic; files her own taxes but feels uncertain about deductions and whether she's compliant.

**Quote**: "In March, I pull together a bunch of documents and just... hope I'm doing it right. I don't know if I'm missing deductions. I don't know if I'm supposed to file quarterly. It's basically anxiety and crossing my fingers."

**Specific example**:
- Last year owed $8K in taxes (unexpected)
- Didn't realize quarterly estimates were a thing until her accountant mentioned it
- For current year, set aside $400/month (guessing)
- Feels like she might be over- or under-saving

**Impact**: Anxiety during tax season; overly conservative savings rate (likely giving up ~$3K in cashflow that she could use)

---

## Emotional Job

Emma wants to feel *prepared* and *compliant*. Right now she feels like she's flying blind.

---

## Jobs to Be Done

**Primary**: Understand tax obligations throughout the year (not just in March)  
**Secondary**: Know what's deductible and optimize deductions

---

## Hypothesis

**Ledger Hypothesis**: "If Emma had quarterly visibility into tax liability, she'd feel more confident and be more aggressive in reinvesting rather than over-saving."

✅ **Strongly validated**:
- She explicitly wants quarterly visibility
- Current workaround is overly conservative
- Would use a tool if it helped her understand obligations

---

## What Emma Would Use

1. Quarterly estimate calculator
2. Deduction tracker (flag what might be deductible)
3. Tax deadline reminder
4. Guide to which filings she needs (federal, state, estimated)

**Impact**: No visibility = poor decision-making; forced into debt; doesn't understand her own business financials

---

**Primary**: Have visibility into income, expenses, and tax liability without doing bookkeeping myself  
**Secondary**: Understand my tax obligations (quarterly estimates, deductions, etc.)

---

## Hypothesis

**Ledger Hypothesis**: "If Rachel had transparent, automated bookkeeping + visibility, she'd understand her finances and avoid surprise tax bills."

✅ **Strongly validated**:
- She explicitly wants transparency + delegation
- Current workaround leaves her blind
- Would use a tool that "keeps books while I focus on therapy"

---

## Segment-Specific Insight

**Professional services with insurance**: Therapists, doctors, counselors have unique needs (insurance deductions, equipment, licensing). They're also often tech-averse and prefer outsourcing. But they still want visibility.

## Summary: Tax & Compliance (Interviews 011-015)

**Validated Insights**:
1. ✅ Solopreneurs: Want quarterly visibility to avoid surprise tax bills
2. ✅ Agencies: Use profit visibility for tax planning and cashflow
3. ✅ Founders: Model strategic decisions (S-Corp, hiring) based on clear profit
4. ✅ All segments fear April surprises; want year-round visibility

**Hypothesis Status**:
- ✅ Validated: Quarterly tax visibility changes behavior (people save proactively)
- ✅ Validated: Clear profit visibility enables strategic tax planning
- ⚠️ Challenged: DIY tax filing is only appealing to small portion; most want expert guidance too
- 🔄 Backlog: Deduction optimization (most owners leave 15-25% on the table)

**Segment Differentiation**:
- **Solopreneurs**: Seek clarity + confidence (don't want surprises)
- **Agencies**: Seek planning tools (model decisions, optimize)
- **Founders**: Seek strategic leverage (tax structure optimization)
- **Professional services**: Seek transparency (understand while outsourcing)
