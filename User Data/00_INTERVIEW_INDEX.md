## How to Read These Interviews

### Option 1: Quick Read (5 minutes per interview)
Read the **Background** + **The Problem** + **What Changed Behavior**. Skip the detailed analysis.

### Option 2: Medium Read (15 minutes per interview)
Read everything except **Follow-Up Questions** and **Product Implications**. Get the full story but skip the analysis.

### Option 3: Deep Read (30 minutes per interview)
Read the entire interview. Understand context, nuance, and product implications.

### Option 4: Narrative Flow (60 minutes)
Read interviews 001 → 002 → 011 as a narrative:
- 001 (Maya): Emotion barrier → why automation matters
- 002 (Raj): Visibility solution → why dashboard matters
- 011 (Emma): Tax anxiety → why quarterly estimates matter

This trio tells a complete story of the three jobs.

## Using This for Client Conversations

### The Pitch (5 minutes)
*"Most teams have customer insights scattered everywhere. Product Brain is a system where every interview feeds a hypothesis, every hypothesis has evidence, and every OKR traces back to what customers actually told us. Here's what it looks like in practice."*

### The Walk-Through (15 minutes)

1. **Show interview 001** (Maya)
   - Raw customer signal: "I don't want to be the annoying person asking for money"
   - Emotional barrier revealed

2. **Show how it appears in Signals Synthesis** 
   - Theme: "Emotion > Features"
   - Pattern: 8 of 10 solopreneurs mention this

3. **Show the JTBD framework**
   - Emily's need: "Make invoicing feel straightforward"
   - Emotional job: "Remove shame from payment follow-up"

4. **Show the hypothesis**
   - "Automation reduces emotional friction"
   - Evidence: Interview 001, 003, 005, 009 all confirm

5. **Show the OKR**
   - Company goal: "Reduce stress of small business finance"
   - This hypothesis directly supports that goal

6. **Conclusion**
   - "This is how we move from 'customers are anxious' to 'we ship features that reduce anxiety' to 'we measure anxiety reduction.'"
