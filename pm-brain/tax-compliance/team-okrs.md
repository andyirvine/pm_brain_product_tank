## Team Mission

Give small business owners a clear, continuously updated view of their tax obligation — so they never face an April surprise, always make confident quarterly payments, and understand how every dollar they earn affects what they keep.

## Team OKRs — 2024

### O1: Eliminate the April Surprise for Ledger users

**Why**: 5/10 TC users were surprised by their tax bill this year. This is the acute pain event that defines the category. If Ledger can make "the April Surprise" something that only happens to non-users, we own the narrative.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: 80% of active TC users have their final tax bill within 15% of their Ledger estimate**
- Baseline: Unknown (pre-launch); industry standard for static estimates is ±40%
- Target: ±15% accuracy for 80% of users by end of first tax cycle (April 2025)
- Measurement: Compare Ledger's Q4 estimate to user's actual filed return (via self-reported survey)

**KR2: 70% of active TC users report "no surprises" at tax filing time**
- Baseline: ~50% of TC interviewees were surprised (national rate is ~30% for self-employed)
- Target: 70% of Ledger users report their tax bill matched expectations
- Measurement: Post-filing survey (April 2025): "Was your final tax bill close to what you expected?"

**KR3: Zero Ledger users incur underpayment penalties in 2024**
- Baseline: 4/10 TC interviewees have incurred penalties at least once in the past 3 years
- Target: 0 penalties for users who follow Ledger's quarterly estimate recommendations
- Measurement: Post-Q4 survey: "Did you incur an IRS underpayment penalty?"
- Note: this is aspirational; we cannot control if users ignore recommendations

---

### O2: Make quarterly tax payments a routine, not a crisis

**Why**: 8/10 TC users use a static prior-year method for quarterly estimates. Variable income makes this method nearly always wrong. A dynamic, real-time estimate removes the uncertainty — and the anxiety.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: 90% of active TC users have their quarterly estimate calculated dynamically (not manually)**
- Baseline: 0% (feature not launched)
- Target: 90% of TC-area users complete quarterly estimate using Ledger's dynamic calculator
- Measurement: Feature adoption tracking; % of users who view estimate before each Q due date

**KR2: Users report 7+/10 confidence in their quarterly estimate**
- Baseline: Most interviewees described "guessing" with 3–4 confidence
- Target: 70% of users rate "7 or higher" when asked: "How confident are you that your current quarterly estimate is accurate?"
- Measurement: In-app survey; quarterly pulse

**KR3: Average time spent calculating quarterly payment drops from ~60 minutes to < 5 minutes**
- Baseline: ~45–90 minutes (research, prior year review, manual calculation)
- Target: Under 5 minutes with Ledger's dynamic estimator
- Measurement: Post-feature launch survey: "How long did it take to calculate your last quarterly payment?"

---

### O3: Build a deduction awareness habit that increases tax efficiency

**Why**: 7/10 TC users believe they're missing deductions, but have no system for capturing them. Deductions identified early are deductions captured — deductions found in March are often already lost.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: Users capture ≥ 10% more in deductions in 2024 vs. their prior year return**
- Baseline: Prior-year deduction total from user self-report
- Target: 10% increase in documented deductions for active deduction-tracker users
- Measurement: Self-reported comparison of 2023 vs. 2024 deduction totals
- Note: Requires deduction tracker to launch by Q3 at latest

**KR2: 60% of active TC users log at least one deduction per week**
- Baseline: 0% (feature not launched)
- Target: 60% weekly active deduction logging
- Measurement: In-app event tracking

**KR3: NPS on "tax preparedness" reaches ≥ 7/10**
- Baseline: Most TC interviewees describe preparedness as 3–5/10
- Target: 70% of users score 7+ on: "How prepared do you feel for tax season right now?"
- Measurement: Monthly NPS pulse

## What We're Not Building in 2024

- **Actual tax filing** — Compliance and liability risk; Ledger plans, doesn't file
- **State tax handling beyond home state** — Multi-state complexity deferred to 2025
- **Entity formation guidance** — S-Corp, LLC recommendations; legal risk; provide data, not advice
- **Done-for-you bookkeeping** — Evidence shows users want understanding, not outsourcing (Bet 8 challenged)
- **AI "tax advisor" chatbot** — Users want transparency + verifiable calculations, not black-box answers
