### ✅ Shift 4: Open Banking Maturing (Opportunity)
As Plaid and open banking APIs improve (99.1% uptime, Section 1033 CFPB rule incoming mid-2024), Ledger can pull in bank, payment, and income data from multiple sources into one view. This makes *genuine* consolidation technically feasible at low cost — and raises the bar for fragmented tools.

Stripe, Square, and PayPal are expanding into invoicing — but critically, **each only sees their own payment rail**. A business running Stripe invoices + PayPal payments + direct bank transfers will still be fragmented across platforms. Ledger's cross-platform consolidation becomes the **layer above** embedded finance, not a competitor to it.

> **Strategic signal**: Position Ledger as *"the view that no single payment platform can give you"* — this turns the embedded finance threat into a consolidation argument.
