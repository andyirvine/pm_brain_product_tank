🟢 Tier 1 · Very High Confidence

Bet 7: Solopreneurs ≠ Agencies

Two distinct segments with fundamentally different needs
Segment A · Solopreneurs

Simplicity & Emotional Relief

Maya, Sarah, Lisa, Priya, Amara, Emma, Rachel, Olivia, Nathan — prioritize speed, ease, and automation that overcomes anxiety.

Need: Simplified defaults, gentle automation, reduced friction
Segment B · Agencies

Visibility & Integration

Raj, James, Tom, Michael, Vikram, Carlos, Sophie, Marcus, Fiona — prioritize team features, data consolidation, and system integration.

Need: Full UI, integrations, multi-user support
Next Action

Build segment-specific onboarding and UX defaults. A/B test simplified vs. full UI by segment in Q2. Measure NPS + activation time by segment.

🟢 Tier 1 · High Confidence

Bet 2: Emotion > Features

Emotional barriers — not functional gaps — are the primary blocker for solopreneurs
"I don't want to be the person who nags people for money; if it's automated, it's okay."
— Maya · Interview 001
"If the system handled it automatically, I'd invoice weekly instead of quarterly."
— Sarah · Interview 003
"I'm anxious about taxes; I want clarity, not features."
— Emma · Interview 011
Root Cause

5+ interviews surfaced shame and anxiety as root causes of underusing financial tools — not missing functionality.

Next Action

Tone/psychology testing on reminders. Default to "helpful tone." Measure anxiety reduction as a product metric.

🟢 Tier 1 · High Confidence

Bet 1: Consolidation Beats Best-of-Breed

8/20 interviews cite friction from 3+ tools — consolidation directly validated
"Invoicing + payment tracking in one place reduced admin by 3–4 hours/month."
— Raj · Interview 002
"Wave + QuickBooks + Stripe is chaos."
— David · Interview 010
"Syncing FreshBooks with Asana + QuickBooks is constant friction."
— Michael · Interview 008
Market Tailwind

Solopreneur economy growing — more income streams = more tool fragmentation = stronger consolidation case (Market Shift 3)

Next: Track "tool consolidation NPS." Measure retention on accounts that expand from invoicing to taxes.
