## Company Vision & Mission

**Vision**: Small business owners feel in control of their money.

**Mission**: Provide a single source of truth where small business owners see cashflow clearly, make confident decisions, and reduce financial stress.

## Company OKRs — 2024

### O1: Reduce the time and stress overhead of small business finance

**Why this objective**: Interviews show owners spend 5-10 hours/month on financial admin. More importantly, they're anxious about their finances. Our job is to eliminate both.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: Owners reduce time spent on invoicing + tax prep by 50%**
- Target: Current baseline: 6-8 hours/month on invoicing + tax prep
- Target: 3-4 hours/month
- Measurement: Post-launch survey + in-app usage data
- How we measure success: Owner reports 50% time reduction vs. previous tool

**KR2: Owners report confidence in their financial position (7+/10 NPS on tax clarity)**
- Current baseline: Most owners report "uncertain" or "anxious" about taxes
- Target: 70% of users report "confident" about tax position
- Measurement: NPS survey: "How confident are you in your tax obligation for 2024?"
- Success: 70% score 7+ (on 1-10 scale)

**KR3: Monthly revenue from Ledger reaches $50K MRR**
- Current baseline: Pre-launch (Seed stage)
- Target: $50K MRR by end of 2024
- Measurement: Stripe/bank dashboard
- Rationale: $50K MRR = sustainable team + proof of model

---

### O2: Own the "cashflow decision-making" layer (not just compliance)

**Why this objective**: Competitors own invoicing or taxes. We own the decision-making layer: "Should I hire? Should I invest in that tool? Can I afford this?" This is our differentiation.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: 80% of users actively use cashflow forecasting or decision-making features**
- Measure: "Opened cashflow dashboard" + "Used hiring/pricing calculator"
- Target: 80% of MAU (Monthly Active Users) use these features
- Success: Not just compliance tool; actively used for decisions

**KR2: Owners report they made better business decisions due to Ledger (6+/10)**
- Measure: Post-feature launch survey: "To what extent has Ledger helped you make better business decisions?"
- Target: 60% score 6+
- Success: Decision-making layer is valuable and differentiated

**KR3: Build and validate 2 proof points of decision enablement (hiring + pricing)**
- Examples:
  - Case study: "Owner used Ledger to model hiring impact; reduced risk"
  - Case study: "Owner used Ledger to benchmark pricing; increased revenue 10%"
- Measurement: Customer story + revenue impact quantified
- Success: Demonstrate real business impact

---

### O3: Establish Ledger as the trusted advisor for small business finance

**Why this objective**: Trust is our moat. If owners trust us with their financial data and decisions, they'll expand to other features, refer others, and stick around.

**Key Results**:

**KR1: NPS ≥ 40 (net promoter score) among active users**
- Current baseline: Pre-launch
- Target: 40+ (industry benchmark: 30-40)
- Measurement: Monthly NPS survey
- Success: Owners actively recommend Ledger

**KR2: Establish accountant partnership program (50+ accountants onboarded)**
- Rationale: Accountants trust us → customers trust us
- Target: 50 accountants using Ledger for client visibility
- Measurement: Partner dashboard sign-ups
- Success: Accountants actively recommend Ledger to clients

**KR3: 25% of new customers come from referral/organic word-of-mouth**
- Current baseline: Pre-launch
- Target: 25% of MRR-generating customers from referrals
- Measurement: SignUp source tracking
- Success: Organic growth driven by trust

## Strategic Bets & Hypotheses

### Bet 1: Consolidation Beats Best-of-Breed

**Hypothesis**: Owners prefer one integrated tool (invoicing + taxes + cashflow) over three specialized tools.

**Evidence from interviews**: 8/20 mention fragmentation friction; 7/20 use 3+ tools for finance

**Validation plan**: 
- Track "tool consolidation NPS" — would you replace other tools with Ledger?
- Measure retention on accounts that expanded from invoicing to taxes

### Bet 2: Emotion > Features

**Hypothesis**: Reducing anxiety and shame is more valuable than adding features.

**Evidence from interviews**: 8+ solopreneurs mention emotional barriers; automation solves psychology, not just function

**Validation plan**:
- Measure emotional outcomes: "I feel less anxious about money"
- Track behavioral changes: Do automated reminders actually change follow-up behavior?
- Tone/psychology A/B tests: Does reminder tone impact openness rate?

### Bet 3: Accountants Are Distribution

**Hypothesis**: Accountants recommending Ledger to clients is more efficient than direct sales.

**Evidence from interviews**: Interview 010 (David, accountant) actively recommends; 3/20 mention strong accountant relationships

**Validation plan**:
- Build accountant view (read-only access for clients)
- Create referral tracking; measure % of customers from accountant referrals
- Launch accountant partner program; target 50 partners by end of 2024

### Bet 4: Decision-Making is Differentiator

**Hypothesis**: Competitors own compliance; we own decision-making (hiring, pricing, growth).

**Evidence from interviews**: 7/20 mention making conservative decisions due to uncertainty; 5/20 want help with business decisions

**Validation plan**:
- Launch hiring impact calculator; measure adoption + usage
- Launch pricing benchmark; measure impact on revenue decisions
- Collect case studies of "business decisions enabled by Ledger"
